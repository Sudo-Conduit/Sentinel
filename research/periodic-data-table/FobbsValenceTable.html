Field Valence Theory · Orbital-Bound Design

Fobbs Valence Table

Group and period fall away. Every orbital block is its own valence mountain — FV climbs from the empty shell up to the half-filled peak, then back down to full, regardless of which column an element sits in. FV = half − |occ − half|
FV — "Fobbs Valence" — is this model's own derived number. It isn't the textbook valence-electron count, even where the two agree.

Field note — the half-filled peak isn't a clean column. Cr and Mo give up their "expected" FV=4 seat early, pulling one electron out of the s-orbital to reach the more stable half-filled d⁵ configuration — landing them at FV=5 alongside Mn · Tc · Re · Bh. Click either group to compare.
