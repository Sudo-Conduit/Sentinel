Auction model · Yahoo Overture style
Keyword/topic-based auction. Highest bidder above the AI-computed floor wins placement. Floor protects against underpricing high-value inventory.

Ads are platform-default with author opt-out. ManifoldAI learns per-page signal weights over time. New pages start at category-average weights.
