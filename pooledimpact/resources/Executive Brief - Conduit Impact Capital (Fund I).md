# **Executive Brief: Conduit Impact Capital – Fund I**

**Strictly Confidential | Prepared for Employee Candidates**

## **1. Fund Mission & Strategy**

**Conduit Impact Capital – Fund I** is a $150M Asset-Backed Finance (ABF) vehicle designed to bridge the gap between high-yield sustainable infrastructure and institutional-grade credit markets. Our objective is to aggregate a portfolio of machine-verified, cash-flowing assets to be exited via a **CUSIP-rated Bond Securitization** in Year 5.

## **2. The "Physics" of the Fund: The 80/10/10 Architecture**

To support a **95% Loan-to-Value (LTV)** Senior Facility, the fund utilizes a proprietary "Sleeve" structure to isolate risk and maximize recovery:

* **Sleeve A (80% - The Core Pool):** Senior-priority royalties from Solar, Geothermal, and Data Center projects. These are the primary collateral for our 95% LTV Senior Lender.
* **Sleeve B (10% - The Alpha Buffer):** Direct equity/operational control in high-margin infrastructure OpCos (e.g., Biodiesel, Grid Balancing). This sleeve provides the yield "spread" and absorbs localized volatility.
* **Sleeve C (10% - The Safety Vault):** Income-producing industrial and residential real estate. This sleeve provides tax depreciation for LPs and secures a **$9.75M HELOC** used as a "Break-Glass" liquidity bridge to keep the Senior Lender current during project-level disruptions.

## **3. Operational Standards & Transparency**

We operate on a "System-of-Record" philosophy. We do not rely on self-reported monthly statements from project managers.

* **4-Hour Data Sync:** Utilizing **Skyvia**, we maintain a live API handshake with SPV accounting cores (QuickBooks/Xero).
* **Bank-to-Book Engine:** Automated reconciliation between bank feeds, accounting ledgers, and physical IoT production sensors (e.g., Solar Inverters).
* **Irrevocable Step-In Rights:** Our **Master Royalty Agreements (MRA)** grant the GP the right to seize SPV accounts and replace onsite management within 72 hours of a technical default or data disconnection.

## **4. The Exit Thesis**

By Year 5, the fund will have 48 months of immutable, machine-verified data. This "Data Seasoning" allows us to convert a portfolio of disparate infrastructure projects into a standardized, investment-grade fixed-income instrument for institutional buyers.
