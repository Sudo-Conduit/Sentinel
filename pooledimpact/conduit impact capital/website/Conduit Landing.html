Market Context

The allocation landscape has shifted.

Institutional capital is rotating into infrastructure credit. The question is no longer whether to allocate — it is how to access structures built for durability, not narrative.

01 — Yield Compression

Traditional fixed income no longer performs

Rates have repriced, but the structural search for yield-plus-stability has not resolved. Infrastructure credit sits in the gap: real assets, contracted cash flows, senior security — with spreads that compensate for illiquidity without compensating for opacity.

02 — Institutional Access

The structures that exist are built for developers

Project finance, tax equity, and development-stage vehicles carry construction risk, equity volatility, and operational complexity that institutional allocators are not positioned to absorb. Conduit Fund I is built for capital deployment, not project development.

03 — Verification Gap

Impact claims are unverifiable at scale

ESG labeling has expanded faster than verification infrastructure. Conduit is built on machine-validated, three-way matched data from day one — not because impact is the thesis, but because verifiable data is how you build a bond-eligible portfolio.

Investment Thesis

Real assets. Contracted cash flows. Senior position.

Pooled Impact is not an ESG fund. It is an asset-backed finance platform that happens to deploy capital into infrastructure that matters — because that is where the most durable yield lives.

The thesis begins with a simple observation: essential infrastructure generates predictable revenue. Solar installations under 10–15 year fixed-price contracts do not renegotiate because oil prices moved.

When the underlying asset is essential, contracted, and senior-secured, the yield is not a promise. It is a structure.

01

Asset-Backed

Every dollar deployed is secured against a physical, revenue-generating infrastructure asset. No unsecured positions. No development-stage exposure.

02

Contracted Revenue

All portfolio assets operate under long-term offtake agreements or capacity contracts. Revenue is not projected — it is scheduled.

03

Senior Facility

Conduit holds the senior credit position across all SPVs. Subordinated capital and equity absorb first loss.

04

Machine Verified

Three-way data matching — bank feed, accounting ledger, IoT sensors — validates every revenue event before it enters the fund record.

05

Structured Exit

48 months of validated operating data positions the portfolio for investment-grade securitization. The exit is built into the origination.
